# ## **The six phases of data analysis**

+ **Ask phase**: understand the challenge to be solved or the question to be  answered.
+ **prepare phase**: find and collect the data you'll need to answer your question. Data source, gather data and verify accurateness and usefulness for answering questions.
+ **Process phase**: clean and organize your data. Missing values, inconsistencies
+ **analyse phase**: do the necessary data analysis to uncover answers and solutions
+ **share phase**: present your findings to decision-makers through a report, presentation, or data visualizations
+ **act phase**: put the data insights into action

## **Iteration during the data analysis process**

Results from each step are the inputs for the next step. It may not be linearly.
