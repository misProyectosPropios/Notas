## Analytical skills

Qualities and characteristics associated with solving problems using facts

+ Curiosity:
	+ Want to learn something
+ Understanding context:
	+ Condition in which something exists or happens. Try to understand if something is in context
+ Technical mindset:
	+ Break things down into smaller steps or pieces and work with them in an orderly and logical way
+ Data design:
	+ How you organize information.
	+ It's related to the database.
+ Data strategy:
	+ Management of people processes and tools used in data analysis

## Analytical thinking

Identifying and defining a problem and then solving it by using data in an organized step by step manner

+ Visualization: 
	+ graphical representation of information
	+ Ex: graphs, maps, etc
+ Strategy: 
	+ see what they ​want to achieve with the data and how they can get there.
+ Problem orientation:
	+ identify, ​describe, and solve problems. Know the goal. A lot of quesitons.
+ correlation:
	+ Find what causes something. Be careful.
	+ Correlation does not equal causation. ​In other words, just because two pieces of ​data are both trending in the same direction, ​that doesn't necessarily mean they are all related
+ Big picture and detail oriented thinking: 
	+ looking at a complete puzzle, not piece by pice.
	+ figuring out all of the aspects ​that will help you execute a plan.
